# Meet an Optimist — question bank

A reservoir of questions to draw from for the newsletter's monthly "Meet an Optimist" section. Pick three per issue, in the voice of the rest of the newsletter — see [newsletter.md](newsletter.md) for the section's format rules (3 Qs + 3 As, first name only, no photo).

When you use a question, log it in [meet-an-optimist-asked.md](meet-an-optimist-asked.md) so we don't repeat the same three in the next issue.

## The twelve

1. What got you to your first meeting?
2. What do you wish more people in Mount Horeb knew about the club?
3. What's something the club has done that meant more than you expected?
4. What's something simple about being an Optimist that people might not expect?
5. What's an Optimist event you look forward to each year?
6. If a friend asked what an Optimist meeting is like, what would you tell them?
7. What do you think will surprise people about their first Optimist meeting?
8. What's the best part of meeting the families in the Someone You Should Know program?
9. What's an Optimist tradition you'd never want to give up?
10. What do you most enjoy about being part of the club?
11. Joining a new club can be hard. How do Mount Horeb Optimists make new members feel right at home?
12. What's a moment from a club event this past year that still makes you smile?
